## Other sprites

The checkpoint flag, coin, heart and ladder (`assets/sprites/`) were made for Ironvale from its concept art. The rest of that folder (sky, castle and hills backdrops, ground and platform tiles, torch, crate, barrel, banner) is only the fallback for a level without a tile map; no stage uses it now.
