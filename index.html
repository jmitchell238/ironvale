Train at the forge between stages

  1. A D / stick — run. W / ↑ on a ladder — climb. Space / JUMP — jump (again in air = double jump).
  2. ATK or J / F / Shift — sword. Keep swinging: the third hit of a chain hits hardest. A hit stops an enemy's wind-up. S / ↓ — duck (you can slash low).
  3. Bone Guards block from the front, so bait their swing or get behind them. Ghosts drift through walls. Wolves are fast but fragile.
  4. Collect coins, grab hearts to heal, pass a flag to checkpoint. Beat every foe to open the gate.
  5. Level-ups bank forge points mid-stage. Spend them between stages.
  6. Clear all four biomes to face the Iron Warden. Then New Game+.
