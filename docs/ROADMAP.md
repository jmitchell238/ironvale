# Roadmap

The campaign goes from bright to dark, one look per stage:

1. **Forgegate Fields**: sunny sea cliffs (Magic Cliffs), ending with the Nightmare (a spectral horse). Done.
2. **Forest Ramparts**: swamp and forest (Gothicvania Swamp), ending with the Hell Beast. Done.
3. **Forge Ruins**: gothic town and church (Gothicvania Town and Church), ending with the Fallen Angel in the nave. Done.
4. **Iron Caverns**: a dark castle and the Iron Warden (Gothicvania Old Dark Castle tiles and Gothic Castle hall, Demon sprite for the boss). Done.

All four stages are built as tile maps with their own theme, and each ends in a boss arena past its gate.
